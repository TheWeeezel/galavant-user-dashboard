GALAVANT — PRESS KIT
====================

Galavant is a walk-to-earn game on the Enjin blockchain. Players earn WATTS by
walking with balance bikes, trade parts and NFT bikes with other players, and
redeem earned WATTS for real ENJ at the end of each season out of a budget
funded by platform revenue.
